About Nusantara Travel

Local knowledge, honest planning, no surprise fees.

Our story

Nusantara Travel started as a small team of guides based in Yogyakarta, helping independent travellers put together itineraries that go beyond the usual tourist route. We've since grown into a full booking service, but the itineraries are still built by people who have actually been to the places we sell.

Our aims

We aim to make trip planning across Indonesia's islands simple — one booking for flights, transfers, accommodation, and guided activities, with a real person to contact if plans change.

Why book with us

  • Local guides in every region we operate in
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden booking fees
  • 24/7 support line for travellers already on a trip
  • Flexible rebooking if your travel dates change
